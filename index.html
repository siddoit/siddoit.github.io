Robotics & Automation Engineer

Hi! I'm a B.Tech Robotics & Automation student at Saintgits College of Engineering, Kottayam. I build intelligent systems at the intersection of AI, Computer Vision, and Embedded Hardware.

Currently interning at Innovizta (AI & IoT) and KEL (robotics R&D), and working on the ICCC Saintgits Taiwan-India Collaboration for distributed sensor networks. My ongoing research focuses on sim-to-real transfer for autonomous vehicles using NVIDIA Isaac Sim and TFLite-based edge inference.

I'm passionate about making robotics practical and deployable — from writing ROS 2 navigation stacks to flashing firmware on ESP32s. When I'm not building things, I write about my learning journey on the blog.
