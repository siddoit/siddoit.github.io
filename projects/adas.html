Ongoing Edge AI Research

Autonomous Vehicle Research Platform

ADAS running on a $400 Galaxy S23 instead of a $2,000 Jetson dev kit, used as the primary edge compute unit.

View on GitHub

Overview

This research project runs Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) on a Samsung Galaxy S23 as the primary edge compute unit for autonomous navigation. The core thesis: capable autonomous navigation doesn't require a $2,000 Jetson dev kit — a flagship phone's NPU is powerful enough for real-time inference.

What I'm Building

A heterogeneous edge-AI pipeline for the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 platform, distributing perception workloads across GPU, NPU, and CPU. Models move from PyTorch tracing through ONNX conversion and are deployed via Qualcomm AI Hub, with INT8/FP16 quantization to keep inference on the NPU.

Technical Highlights

  • Edge deployment — static input dimensions, delegate compatibility, and memory/thermal tuning on mobile silicon.
  • NVIDIA Isaac Sim — synthetic datasets for sim-to-real transfer of driving policies.
  • Sensor fusion — phone camera and IMU for sub-50ms inference at 30 FPS without CPU fallback.
  • Model tooling — PyTorch tracing and ONNX conversion for computer-vision models.
